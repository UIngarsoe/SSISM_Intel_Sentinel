## When You Read the Global New Light of Myanmar

When you read the 
Global New Light of Myanmar (GNLM), it often looks calm and normal.

You see news like:

- Students receiving awards  
- Doctors helping people  
- Archaeology work in Bagan  
- Drug seizures  
- Government meetings with foreign companies  
- Historical articles about World War II and Russia  

It feels like:

> “The country is functioning normally.”

But at the same time, other reports show:

- Airstrikes increasing across the country  
- Large amounts of jet fuel entering Myanmar  
- Secret shipping routes bringing fuel for military aircraft  

These two stories are not separate.

They are connected.

---

## 🎭 Two Faces of the Same System

The government shows **one face** in the newspaper.

Behind that, there is **another reality** on the ground.

| What GNLM Shows | What Is Happening Behind |
|---|---|
| History, culture, students, doctors | Airstrikes, war, destruction |
| Drug seizures, inspections | Military control and fear |
| Foreign investment meetings | Need for money and legitimacy |
| Calm, normal daily life | Ongoing civil conflict |

This is done on purpose.

The newspaper tries to make people feel:

> “Everything is under control. The state is stable.”

---

## 🧠 Why Use World War II Stories?

GNLM talks about Russia’s “Great Patriotic War” in WWII.

This is a way of saying:

> “We are like those who fought fascism and colonialism. We are protecting the country.”

It borrows **historical hero stories** to justify what is happening today.

---

## ✈️ The Hidden Part: Jet Fuel for Airstrikes

While the newspaper talks about peace and culture, reports show:

- Fuel coming from China
- Fuel coming through Thailand
- “Ghost ships” hiding their tracks at sea

This fuel keeps military aircraft flying.

And those aircraft are used in air attacks inside the country.

This is the part you will **never** see in GNLM.

---

## 🧩 Why Show Archaeology, Students, and Trains?

During conflict, the newspaper highlights:

- Bagan excavations
- Outstanding students
- Train services
- Medical teams

This creates a feeling of **normal life continuing**.

It distracts people from thinking about the war.

---

## ⚖️ A Legal Front Outside Myanmar

A legal case has been opened in Timor-Leste using international law.

While the newspaper talks about history and peace, others are opening **legal cases** about airstrikes.

---

## ✅ What Civilians Should Understand

When you read GNLM:

Do not read only what is written.

Ask:

- What is **not** being shown?
- Why is the paper full of “normal life” stories during war?
- Why use old war history to explain today?

Because:

> The newspaper shows the **mask**.  
> The jet fuel and airstrikes show the **reality**.

Both are part of the same system.

---

## 🧭 Simple Rule for Readers

If the news looks **too calm** during a time of conflict,  
that calmness is part of the message.
