### Abstract

This briefing introduces Version 4 of the SSISM Forensic Engine — a structured, reproducible analytical framework designed to help civil society, independent researchers, and policy analysts evaluate the quality and potential long-term risks of policy recommendations concerning Myanmar’s international relations, particularly with ASEAN.

The engine emphasizes transparency, logical coherence, and evidence-based assessment rather than ideological positioning.

---

### 1. Purpose and Scope

Myanmar’s complex political transition generates diverse policy proposals, ranging from engagement to isolationist approaches. SSISM V4 provides a neutral methodological tool to assess such recommendations by examining their empirical grounding, linguistic patterns, strategic risks, and historical alignment.

The goal is to support **truth-seeking scholarship** and informed decision-making by civil society and academic communities.

---

### 2. Core Components of SSISM V4 Forensic Engine

#### 2.1 Digital Trust Score (Φ)
A logistic regression-based model that evaluates policy recommendations across multiple dimensions:
- Source authority and independence
- Empirical grounding and falsifiability
- Linguistic patterns (performative vs. substantive)
- Historical precedent alignment
- Risk of unintended consequences (e.g., institutional delay, economic isolation)

**Score Interpretation**:
- Φ > 0.7: High substantive quality
- Φ 0.3–0.7: Moderate — requires further scrutiny
- Φ < 0.3: High risk of being primarily performative or strategically counterproductive

#### 2.2 Mandatory Review & Archiving Protocol
Recommendations scoring below a defined threshold trigger systematic forensic archiving with full text preservation and SHA-256 cryptographic hashing for long-term scholarly transparency.

#### 2.3 Forensic Archive
All evaluated items are stored with complete metadata, enabling future longitudinal academic studies on policy discourse during Myanmar’s transition period.

### 3. Application: Evaluating Isolationist Policy Advice

Recent proposals advocating hardened isolationist postures toward ASEAN (“Clenched Fist” approach) or withdrawal from regional mechanisms can be systematically assessed using this framework. Key evaluation dimensions include:

- Strategic risk of deepened economic isolation
- Potential increase in dependency on non-ASEAN actors
- Historical correlation between prolonged isolation and internal militarization
- Balance between short-term narrative control and measurable long-term stability outcomes

### 4. Guidance for Civil Society and Independent Researchers

**Recommended Analytical Practices**:
1. Apply the “SAY vs. NOT SAY” method when reading official statements and policy proposals.
2. Evaluate recommendations through the Digital Trust Score lens.
3. Prioritize verifiable evidence over narrative framing.
4. Maintain methodological transparency and reproducibility.

This framework supports rigorous, non-partisan analysis while strengthening the overall quality of public discourse.

The SSISM V4 Forensic Engine represents a methodological contribution toward more structured and transparent evaluation of policy advice in complex transition environments.
