## 1. Mathematical Quantification of Economic Distress

$$\text{Burden Index} = \sum_{i} w_i \cdot \ln\left(\frac{P_{\text{current}, i}}{P_{\text{baseline}, i}}\right)$$
Where:
* $P_{\text{current}, i}$ = Present market price of staple item $i$.
* $P_{\text{baseline}, i}$ = Historical benchmark price of staple item $i$.
