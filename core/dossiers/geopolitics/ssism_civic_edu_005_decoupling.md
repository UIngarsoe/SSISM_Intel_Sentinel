## 1. The Three Pillars of Local Implementation
To decouple successfully on the ground, local networks must build and maintain parallel structures across three distinct sectors:
### Pillar 1: Administrative Autonomy (ဒေသန္တရ တရားစီရင်ရေးနှင့် အုပ်ချုပ်ရေး)
*   **Action:** Completely bypass the central regime's legal channels, courts, and decrees. 
*   **On-the-Ground Execution:** Establish interim neighborhood, village, and regional dispute resolution councils. All land records, birth registries, and communal contracts should be managed locally and recorded using decentralized ledger systems. Treat the central command's legal threats as structurally irrelevant.
### Pillar 2: Fiscal & Transactional Secession (ဘဏ္ဍာရေးနှင့် စီးပွားရေး လွတ်မြောက်မှု)
*   **Action:** Starve the central state machinery of economic leverage by shifting away from the central banking system.
*   **On-the-Ground Execution:** 
    *   Minimize reliance on the central fiat currency (MMK) in active conflict or liberated zones.
    *   Transition local market trade to alternative units of account, cross-border digital wallets, or neighboring regional currencies.
    *   Route local tax generation directly back into immediate community services (security, medical supplies, education) rather than letting resources leak back to central ministries.
### Pillar 3: Civil Infrastructure Self-Reliance (အရပ်ဘက် အခြေခံအဆောက်အအုံ)
*   **Action:** Establish alternative networks for essential public services to survive state-sponsored blackouts or blockades.
*   **On-the-Ground Execution:** Maintain community-run healthcare clinics, parallel mobile learning centers, and decentralized local mesh networks or satellite communications to sustain independent informational flows.
