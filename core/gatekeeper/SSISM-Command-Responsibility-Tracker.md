# SSISM Command Responsibility Tracker v1.0

**Purpose:**  
A systematic tool to map Myanmar military leaders to patterns of command responsibility, institutional behavior, and systemic impact.

---

## Core Methodology

Every personality analysis should include:

### 1. Command Responsibility Score (0–10)

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|--------|
| 8–10  | Direct, sustained oversight of widespread violations |
| 6–7.9 | Significant involvement in repressive apparatus |
| 4–5.9 | Indirect or mid-level responsibility |
| <4    | Limited or peripheral role |

### 2. Key Tracking Categories

- **Airstrikes & Civilian Targeting**
- **Forced Conscription & Child Soldiers**
- **Surveillance & Intelligence Operations**
- **Torture & Interrogation Networks**
- **Desertion Crackdowns**
- **Public Justification of Violence**

---

## Template Usage

**Command Responsibility Snapshot** (Example)

**General Ye Win Oo**

## Strategic Value

This tracker helps:
- Document individual accountability for future justice processes
- Identify doctrine shifts (e.g., combat → intelligence-centric)
- Assess risks to resistance and civilians
- Support long-term pattern recognition
