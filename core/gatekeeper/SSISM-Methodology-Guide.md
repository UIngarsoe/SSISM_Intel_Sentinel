# SSISM Methodology Guide

**SSISM v1.0** — Spatial-Structural Institutional Systemic Mapping

An independent analytical framework designed to understand Myanmar’s complex power dynamics through systematic, structural, and verifiable methods.

---

## Core Philosophy

SSISM rejects simple surface narratives. Instead, it maps **systems, structures, and institutional behaviors** over time.

We analyze through **two primary lenses**:

- **Mask Line (Presidential / Civilian Facade)**
- **Iron Line (Institutional / Coercive Military Core)**

---

## Key Analytical Concepts

### 1. Duality Trap
The central concept of SSISM. The regime operates through two parallel logics that are interdependent yet in constant tension:
- **Mask Line**: Seeks external legitimacy, diplomatic recognition, and "normal government" appearance.
- **Iron Line**: Maintains doctrinal purity, internal loyalty, and coercive control through violence.

### 2. Compensation Effect
When the Mask Line softens rhetoric for diplomatic gain, the Iron Line hardens kinetic operations to prevent internal morale collapse.

### 3. Rhetoric–Operation Divergence
The greater the gap between official statements in Naypyidaw and actions on the ground, the higher the internal systemic stress.

### 4. Volatile Equilibrium
The current system is stable enough not to collapse immediately, but structurally fragile and high-maintenance.

---

## SSISM Analytical Process

1. **Identify the Dual Lines** (Mask vs Iron)
2. **Map Rhetoric vs Operations**
3. **Assess Institutional Tension**
4. **Evaluate Long-term Systemic Risks**
5. **Provide Verifiable Evidence + Strategic Implications**
