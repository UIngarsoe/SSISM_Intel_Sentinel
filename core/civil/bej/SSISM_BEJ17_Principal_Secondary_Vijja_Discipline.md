---

## 🔍 Core Teaching: Principal vs Secondary

Before any physical action or spoken word arises, the mind has already moved.  

- **Mano Kamma** (mental action) is principal.  
- **Kāya Kamma** (physical action) and **Vacī Kamma** (speech) are secondary manifestations.  

True discipline therefore begins with the mind. Guard the mind first, and the rest follows more naturally.

---

## 📊 Pattern Recognition

When strong emotions arise — whether joy, sorrow, anger, or longing — we are given a field for practice.  

Ask gently:  
- What is principal in this moment?  
- What is secondary?  
- Where is the mind clinging?  
- What can be released with kindness?

This simple inquiry transforms reactive patterns into opportunities for awareness.

## 📌 Vijja Discipline

Observe first.  
Verify second.  
Interpret third.  
React last.

This sequence protects analytical integrity and cultivates wisdom.

## ☸️ Buddha Heart Reflection

All conditioned phenomena are impermanent (*Anicca*). Feelings arise and pass. By meeting them with mindfulness (*Sati*) rather than identification, we move from ignorance (*Avijja*) toward clear knowing (*Vijja*).

The path is not to eliminate human feeling, but to hold it with clarity and compassion.

---

## 🧠 Poormanmeism – The Philosophy of Humble Learning

This path begins with honest declaration:  
“I do not fully know.”

From this ground of intellectual humility, genuine learning and compassion can arise. The practitioner does not claim certainty. He remains a lifelong student — open, respectful, and willing to learn.
