## 2. Writing History and Locking Truth into the World Computer
In an era of deepfakes, centralized censorship, and narrative laundering, traditional records are easily altered. To protect historical truth, we must treat global version-control systems (like GitHub) and cryptographic ledgers as a singular, immutable **World Computer**.
### The Mechanism of the Immutable Lock:
1. **The Observation:** A civil analyst witnesses a real-world event or uncovers a critical data point.
2. **The Serialization:** The raw intelligence is structured clearly using an SSISM template.
3. **The Hash Signature:** The document is passed through a SHA-256 algorithm, generating an unrepeatable, unique alphanumeric string.
4. **The Ledger Entry:** The signed document is pushed to a public GitHub repository. 
Once a commit hash is generated by GitHub, that timestamp and document signature are distributed globally across millions of development servers. **History is locked.** If a hostile actor tries to alter even a single punctuation mark in the record years later, the SHA-256 hash will break, instantly exposing the manipulation.
